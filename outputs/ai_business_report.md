# AI Business Analysis Report

- Total sales operations generated ₹56,155,000 in revenue from 16,862 leads, achieving an overall marketing ROI of 566.96%.
- Referrals delivered the highest revenue (₹17,173,000) and the highest marketing ROI (2,539.97%) with the lowest customer acquisition cost of ₹2,182.89.
- The West region generated the highest revenue at ₹18,519,000, while the North region achieved the highest marketing ROI at 748.94% and highest lead-to-customer conversion rate at 6.74%.
- Google Ads had the highest customer acquisition cost at ₹28,120.25 and lowest marketing ROI at 105.31%, while Central and South regions exhibited elevated acquisition costs exceeding ₹12,000 per customer.

## Risks and Red Flags

- Google Ads generated 2,798 leads and ₹4,561,000 in revenue but required ₹2,221,500 in spend, resulting in a customer acquisition cost of ₹28,120.25 and a marketing ROI of only 105.31%.
- Email Campaign converted only 2.62% of leads to customers, with a low win rate of 15.40% and low revenue per lead of ₹1,710.84.
- South and Central regions demonstrated high customer acquisition costs of ₹12,228.40 and ₹12,016.89 respectively, combined with lower marketing ROIs of 418.63% and 336.49%.

## Regional Analysis

- West led in lead generation (4,727 leads), deals closed (270), and total revenue (₹18,519,000), utilizing 1,956.9 sales hours.
- North produced ₹13,583,000 in revenue from 3,590 leads, achieving the highest regional lead-to-customer rate (6.74%) and the highest regional ROI (748.94%) with ₹1,600,000 in marketing spend.
- South recorded ₹10,274,000 in revenue from 3,251 leads but had the highest regional CAC (₹12,228.40) and highest cost per lead (₹609.35).
- Central had the lowest marketing ROI among all regions at 336.49%, generating ₹7,763,000 in revenue with a win rate of 18.73% and CAC of ₹12,016.89.
- East had the lowest total volume (1,939 leads and ₹6,016,000 revenue) but maintained a balanced performance with a 6.29% lead-to-customer rate and a CAC of ₹7,122.95.

- LinkedIn was the largest lead volume driver (3,754 leads), producing ₹14,564,000 in revenue, 245 deals, and a 6.53% lead-to-customer rate on a spend of ₹2,454,500.

## Business Recommendations

- Evaluate Google Ads acquisition economics to address its customer acquisition cost of ₹28,120.25 and low marketing ROI of 105.31%.
- Investigate the factors behind Referral's strong performance (11.49% lead-to-customer rate and ₹2,182.89 CAC) to assess if referral volume can be scaled.
- Review sales processes and lead qualification in Central and South to understand drivers behind CAC values exceeding ₹12,000 and win rates around 18.73% to 20.28%.
- Examine email campaign content, targeting, and demo scheduling workflows to address the low demo conversion rate of 16.99% and win rate of 15.40%.

## Data Limitations

- The dataset provides aggregate metrics per region and lead source without time-series breakdown, preventing the analysis of trends over time or seasonality.
- There is no cross-tabulation between regions and lead sources, limiting visibility into which channels drive performance within specific territories.
- Customer deal sizes, individual sales rep performance, and post-sale retention or churn data are not included in the dataset.
